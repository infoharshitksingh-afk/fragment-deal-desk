Sources

The pages Deal Desk reads every few days instead of searching at random. Each source earns a weight from what it produces: accounts kept, Tier 1, contacted and replied, pulled toward a starting guess until it has a record. Higher weight means it is checked more often and first. Only content that is new since the last check goes to Claude.

Model

Who Fragment sells to

The scoring model behind every account, built from Fragment's public positioning: its October 2026 Series A ("turn agents into financial operators"), customer stories and the Ledgers, Payments and Recon products. Two target groups: vertical AI companies whose agents run billing, payments and accounting, and fintechs and platforms with multi-directional fund flows. Buyers are engineering and product teams. Hiring is not a signal. Fit is out of 50, timing is out of 50. Tier 1 needs fit of 35 or more and timing of 20 or more. Edit api/_lib/icp.js to retune.

Memory

Company registry

Every company the agent has ever seen, kept or not. Each run checks this list first, so the same company is never added or researched twice. Excluded companies stay here with the reason.
